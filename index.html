The Getaway

Space to spread out, near the water

Tucked about a mile from High Port Marina and a ten-minute walk to Lake Texoma, this custom half-acre property pairs a three-bedroom main house with a private, separate cabin — room enough for families, friend groups, or a full house of guests who each want their own quiet corner.

Fully fenced yard for kids and pets, a fire pit for slow evenings, four smart TVs for movie nights, and fast WiFi throughout. Everything here is built for unplugging without actually losing your comforts.
